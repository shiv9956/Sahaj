# SAHAJ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS) & EXPLAINERS

## FAQ-01: Education loan mein moratorium ka matlab kya hota hai?
- **Answer:** Moratorium padhai ke dauran diya jaane wala ek grace period hai. Isme padhai khatam hone + 6 mahine tak aapko principal repayment nahi karni padti.
- **Source:** doc_edu_rules_v1

## FAQ-02: Kya moratorium ke dauran interest lagta hai?
- **Answer:** Haan, moratorium ke dauran interest accrue hota hai. Aap ya to simple interest har mahine pay kar sakte hain, ya wo interest loan principal mein jod (capitalize) kar diya jata hai.
- **Source:** doc_edu_rules_v1

## FAQ-03: Co-applicant kyu zaroori hota hai?
- **Answer:** Students ki apni income nahi hoti. Isliye bank co-applicant (mummy, papa ya guardian) ki income aur credit score ke aadhar par loan approve karta hai.
- **Source:** doc_edu_rules_v1

## FAQ-04: Insurance mein Free-Look period kya hota hai?
- **Answer:** Policy milne ke 30 dino ke andar agar aapko terms pasand nahi aati hain, to aap policy cancel karke poora premium refund le sakte hain.
- **Source:** doc_suraksha_term_v1
