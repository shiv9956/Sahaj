# Paytm Suraksha Term Life Protect

## Overview
Pure term life insurance policy providing financial security to dependents in case of untimely death of the primary earner or co-applicant.

## Key Terms
- **Sum Assured Range:** ₹25,00,000 to ₹1,00,000,00
- **Policy Term:** 10 to 40 years
- **Entry Age:** 18 to 60 years
- **Free-Look Period:** 30 days from policy receipt

## Exclusions & Waiting Period
- Suicide exclusion within first 12 months.
- Pre-existing medical conditions must be declared.
