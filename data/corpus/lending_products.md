# Paytm EduScholar Premier Education Loan

## Overview
Paytm EduScholar Premier Education Loan is tailored for students pursuing higher education in premier institutions across India. It covers 100% of tuition fees and living expenses with a flexible moratorium period.

## Key Terms
- **Min Loan Amount:** ₹1,00,000
- **Max Loan Amount:** ₹20,00,000
- **Interest Rate Range:** 8.5% to 10.5% p.a. (Floating)
- **Tenure:** 12 to 84 months
- **Moratorium Period:** Course duration + 6 months
- **Processing Fee:** ₹1,000 (Fixed)
- **Co-applicant Requirement:** Mandatory (Parent or Guardian)
- **Collateral:** Not required for loans up to ₹7,50,000

## Eligibility Criteria
- Applicant must be an Indian citizen admitted to a recognized higher education degree/diploma program.
- Co-applicant must have a minimum net monthly income of ₹25,000.
- Credit score of co-applicant should meet partner risk criteria (700+).

## Fees and Charges
- **Processing Fee:** ₹1,000 flat
- **Prepayment Penalty:** 0% after 6 EMI payments
- **Late Payment Charge:** 2% per month on overdue EMI

## Repayment and Moratorium Rules
- During the moratorium period, student can choose simple interest payment or capitalize interest into principal.
- Moratorium interest accrues monthly.

## Documents Required
1. Admission Letter from Institute with Fee Structure.
2. Co-applicant Income Proof (3 months salary slip / 2 years ITR).
3. KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN of applicant and co-applicant).

## Exclusions and Watch-outs
- Moratorium interest accrual increases total repayment amount if capitalized.
- Prepayment allowed without penalty only after 6 months.
